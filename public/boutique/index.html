Interiors for hotels and resorts

Studio Boutique is an interior design practice working in hotels, resorts, and the rooms attached to them. What we pay attention to is how a space behaves once it is full of people: where they sit, what they touch, how long they stay, and which corner they quietly avoid. Most clients come to us already holding a feeling they want and no vocabulary for it yet.

So that is where we start, before anything gets drawn. What the room should do at seven in the morning, and what it should do at midnight. Who it is for, and who it honestly is not for. The drawings come later and they change constantly, but that first conversation usually survives the whole project.

Clients usually arrive with a feeling and no words for it. Much of the early work is just talking until the right words turn up, which is slower than it sounds and saves months further down.

After that we stay close. Early visioning, the model shop, the argument about the lobby carpet at eleven at night, the material swap three weeks before opening. We would rather be in the room for the small decisions than hand over a set of drawings and hope.
