001 — What we do

We make brands that hold a pose and still know how to move.

Action Figure has been building brands since 2016 — strategy, identity, digital, motion and the code underneath. We work with a small number of partners a year and we build every part of the thing, not a deck about it.

The name is the method. A brand that only works in one position is a statue. We design systems with joints in them: enough structure to stay recognisable, enough give to be put into a shape nobody planned for.

004 — Studio

Fourteen people, one bench, no floors between us.

We are strategists, designers, animators and engineers who sit close enough to argue. Everything is prototyped early and badly, then made properly once it has earned it.

We take on eight partners a year. That number is not a positioning exercise — it is the number of things fourteen people can do well.
